## Legal framing for the Netherlands (reuse established Wbetz pattern)
No KSA (Kansspelautoriteit) license confirmed for Rakoo Casino - this places it in the same
position as Wbetz: illegal for the *operator* to offer services to Dutch residents under the Wet
Kansspelen op Afstand, but not a criminal offence for the individual player to use it. No
connection to CRUKS (the Dutch self-exclusion register) follows from the missing KSA license -
a player self-excluded via CRUKS from legal Dutch operators could technically still register here.
Frame this honestly and prominently, not as a buried disclaimer, consistent with how Wbetz was
handled.
