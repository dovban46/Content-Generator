## Legal/regulatory framing for Italy section
Italy's online gambling market is one of the more mature regulated European markets: ADM (Agenzia
delle Dogane e dei Monopoli, formerly AAMS) licenses and supervises online casino, poker, and sports
betting operators. A genuine ADM concession (like 15099 here) means the operator is subject to
Italian consumer-protection rules, self-exclusion registers (RUA - Registro Unico degli
Autoesclusi), and advertising restrictions. This is a materially different, more reassuring
starting point than the offshore-only brands covered elsewhere in this project - frame it as a
positive trust signal, while still being honest that exact operational figures (game count, some
payment/support specifics) are inconsistently reported across secondary sources.
